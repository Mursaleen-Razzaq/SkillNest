Tips for Writing Professional Emails

1. Write a Clear Subject

Make your subject short and specific so the recipient understands the purpose.

2. Be Respectful

Use a suitable greeting, polite language, and a professional closing.

3. Keep It Relevant

Focus on your purpose. Avoid unnecessary details and proofread before sending.
