Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how SkillNest handles information when you visit and use our website.

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SkillNest. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit or use our website.

SkillNest provides online tools and educational resources for students, job seekers, graduates, and professionals.

By using SkillNest, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the website.

2. Information We Collect

The information handled by SkillNest depends on how you use the website.

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when using features such as contact forms or other interactive tools.

This information may include:

  • Name and email address.
  • Messages and feedback you choose to send.
  • Information entered into online tools.

Automatically Collected Information

Our hosting provider or third-party services may process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and access logs.

The exact information collected depends on the services enabled on the website.

3. How Our Tools Handle Your Information

Some SkillNest tools use JavaScript and your browser to calculate results, generate content, or save drafts.

Certain tools may store information locally in your browser using LocalStorage. This can include:

  • Resume builder information.
  • Cover letter drafts.
  • Job application records.
  • Study planner information.
  • Flashcards and task lists.
  • Theme preferences and tool settings.

Important: Browser LocalStorage is stored on your device. It is not automatically sent to SkillNest servers. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may also be able to access locally stored data.

Avoid entering passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into general-purpose tools.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Providing requested tool functionality.
  • Responding to messages and feedback.
  • Improving website usability and performance.
  • Identifying and fixing technical problems.
  • Protecting the website against misuse.

We do not claim to use personal information for purposes that are not described in this policy.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SkillNest or services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, preferences, analytics, or advertising.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. They may help a website remember settings or understand how visitors use it.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

6. Third-Party Services

SkillNest may use third-party services to host, maintain, analyze, or monetize the website.

These services may include:

  • Website hosting: Hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver website pages.
  • Analytics: If enabled, analytics services may collect information about website usage.
  • Advertising: If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.
  • External websites: Links to third-party websites are governed by the privacy policies of those websites.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party providers. Please review their policies before using their services.

7. Advertising and Google AdSense

SkillNest may display advertisements through third-party advertising services in the future.

If Google AdSense is used, Google and its partners may use cookies or other technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices through:

  • Google Advertising Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

Advertising technologies are not necessarily active on SkillNest at all times.

8. Data Sharing

SkillNest does not claim to sell visitors' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website and its users.

9. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid submitting sensitive information through general contact forms or online tools.

10. Data Retention and Local Storage

Information stored in your browser may remain there until you delete it or clear your browser's site data.

If you use an email application through a contact link, your message may be handled and retained by your email provider.

Technical logs and information processed by hosting or third-party services may be retained according to those providers' policies and applicable requirements.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable actions.

You can also:

  • Clear SkillNest data from your browser storage.
  • Manage cookies through browser settings.
  • Avoid providing unnecessary personal information.
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

12. Children's Privacy

SkillNest is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

13. International Visitors

SkillNest may be accessed from different countries. Hosting providers and other service providers may process technical information in locations different from your own.

Your use of the website may therefore involve information processing across national borders, subject to applicable law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, services, or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact SkillNest.

Visit our Contact Us page to send a message.
