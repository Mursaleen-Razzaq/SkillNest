How can I report a problem with a tool?

Select Technical Issue in the contact form and explain the problem, including which tool you were using.

SkillNest is being developed around accessible online tools. Check the individual tool pages for any applicable usage conditions.

Does SkillNest provide career services?

SkillNest provides self-service career tools and educational resources. It does not guarantee employment or job placement.

Does the contact form store my message?

No. This version prepares an email using your device's email application. It does not send or store messages on a SkillNest server.
