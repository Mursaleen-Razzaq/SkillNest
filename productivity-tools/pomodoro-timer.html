How Does the Pomodoro Technique Work?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method that divides work into focused sessions separated by short breaks.

  • Choose one task you want to complete.
  • Start a 25-minute focus session.
  • Work on your selected task without unnecessary distractions.
  • Take a 5-minute short break after a focus session.
  • After four completed focus sessions, take a longer break.
