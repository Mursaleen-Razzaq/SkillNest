Why Use a To-Do List?

A to-do list helps you organize your responsibilities and remember important activities. Instead of keeping everything in your mind, write tasks down and complete them one by one.

  • Break large activities into smaller tasks.
  • Set priorities for important work.
  • Set due dates to manage deadlines.
  • Track completed activities and progress.
  • Review your remaining tasks regularly.
