How to Use Flashcards

Flashcards are a simple learning method. Write a question on one side and its answer on the other. Try to answer the question before revealing the answer.

  • Create short questions about important topics.
  • Write clear and simple answers.
  • Try to recall the answer before flipping the card.
  • Shuffle your cards to practice in a different order.
  • Review difficult questions more frequently.
