Why Use an Online Word Counter?

A word counter helps you understand the length and structure of your writing. It is useful for assignments, essays, reports, articles, and professional documents.

  • Words: Counts words separated by whitespace.
  • Characters: Counts all characters, including spaces.
  • Sentences: Estimates sentences using common punctuation.
  • Paragraphs: Counts non-empty text blocks.
  • Reading time: Estimates reading time using 200 words per minute.
  • Speaking time: Estimates speaking time using 130 words per minute.
