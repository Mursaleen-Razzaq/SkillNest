How does Exam Countdown work?

Add the name, subject, and date of your exam. SkillNest calculates the number of calendar days remaining until the examination.

Exams scheduled for today will display "Today". Past examination dates will be displayed as completed.

Your examination schedule is saved locally in your browser.
